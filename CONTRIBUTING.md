# Contributing to HerMap

Help make community awareness accessible, careful and useful for women in India. Small, well-tested improvements are welcome.

1. Read the README and development guide. For a large change, open an issue explaining the problem, intended behaviour and privacy implications first.
2. Work on a branch and keep changes focused. Follow the existing PHP `inc` framework and put CSS, JS and images under `assets/`.
3. Use fictional fixtures only. Never copy real submissions, account handles, receipts, logs, `.env` values or moderator credentials into source control or public discussions.
4. Run relevant checks. Explain the behaviour changed, how you validated it, and any limitations in your pull request. Include screenshots for visual changes without private data.
5. Keep source and asset attribution. Geography must preserve the official Indian external outline; source dates, transformations and limitations must be documented.

## Product and communication principles

- Viewing and reporting stay free. No payment, advertisement or popularity metric should influence moderation.
- Do not publish identifying details or individual allegations. Public reporting is aggregated, moderated, delayed and thresholded.
- Do not describe report counts as proven crime rates or definitive safe/unsafe rankings. Explain missing data and uncertainty.
- Respect survivors’ choices. Use calm language, avoid victim-blaming and do not solicit stories in public issues.
- Keyboard access, mobile layouts, readable language and reduced-motion preferences matter.
- No new tracking, external data processors, public venue/account scores or collection of personal information without documented review.

Writers, translators, marketers and influencers can contribute explainers, accessibility feedback, responsible awareness materials and outreach ideas through issues. Verify support links with their official providers and keep demonstrations fictional. Contact maintainers before representing HerMap as a partner or making commitments in its name.

Vulnerabilities belong in private email to help@hermap.in; see SECURITY.md. Be respectful of contributors and communities. Contributions must be your own or have documented permission and compatible licensing. Original-project licensing remains a maintainer decision.
